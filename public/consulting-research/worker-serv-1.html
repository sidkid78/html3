01 // AI Readiness Assessment

Strategic advisory practices evaluate enterprise AI maturity across five core dimensions scored from Level 1 (Ad Hoc) to Level 5 (Transformational):

  • Strategy & Portfolio: P&L alignment, stage-gate attrition, CapEx/OpEx ratio dedicated to AI vs. legacy IT.
  • Data Estate: Data lakehouse maturity (Delta Lake, Iceberg), vector databases, metadata lineage, pipeline downtime (MTTD/MTTR).
  • Tech Stack (MLOps): Standard registries (MLflow, W&B), automated CI/CD/CT, containerized orchestration (K8s), GPU efficiency.
  • Talent: Ratio of Data/MLOps Engineers to Data Scientists (target: 3:1), agile pod velocity.

Multi-Year TCO Vectors:

Models factor in inference tokenomics (PTUs vs. pay-per-token vs. self-hosted runtimes), data pipeline compute, PEFT/LoRA re-tuning, and human-in-the-loop (HITL) validation queues.
